Last week, I headed to Saarbrücken, Germany for the ELLIS TRAID Summer School. It was a great week packed with talks and workshops on trustworthy AI in drug discovery. As we rely more on AI to find new drugs, figuring out how to make these models interpretable and robust is getting pretty crucial.

Outside the main lectures, I also brought a poster to share some of my own ongoing work. I presented **Bonder**, an E(3)-invariant graph neural network I've been building to reconstruct molecules directly from 3D atomic clouds, predicting bond orders and formal charges. It was a project that branched out from my Master's thesis, and I was happy to hear that other atendees found uses for it.

It is still a work in progress, but you can [check out the poster and related materials here]({{ '/posters/traid2026/' | relative_url }}).
