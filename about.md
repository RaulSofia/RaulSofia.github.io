I am an **AI researcher** and **machine learning engineer** focused on **deep learning**, **3D geometric reasoning**, and artificial intelligence for **complex spatial problems**.

My academic path began with **two BSc** degrees **pursued simultaneously**: a **BSc in Biochemistry** and a **BSc in Informatics Engineering**. This combination allowed me to develop complementary perspectives on biological systems, computation, and engineering. I later completed an **MSc in Data Science and Engineering**, where I specialized further in machine learning and artificial intelligence.

During this time, I began conducting research at **LARN**, a research group within [CISUC](https://www.cisuc.uc.pt/), under the supervision of [Prof. Joel Arrais](https://joelarrais.github.io/). My work has focused on **deep learning**, **generative models**, **computer vision**, and **3D geometry**, with **computational drug design** and molecular modeling serving as the main application domains. In particular, I am interested in developing AI systems that can learn from structured spatial data and reason about complex three-dimensional biological systems.

Although much of my research so far has been applied to drug discovery, my broader interests extend to other areas involving **3D understanding** and **spatial perception**. I am particularly interested in future work involving **computer vision**, **autonomous navigation**, and **3D mapping**, while continuing to explore applications in molecular modeling and computational drug design.

Outside research, I enjoy electronics and hardware projects, occasionally play basketball, and previously competed in the International Biology Olympiad.
